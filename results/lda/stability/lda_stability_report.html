LDA topic-number selection and stability report

Similarity metric throughout: cosine between aligned P(day-type | topic) distributions (Hungarian matching to the published reference).

Data: reconciled CNIO/DB1 — data/processed/lda/user_embeddings_from_pkl.csv. Reference model unchanged (LDA_model_vdec25.gensim).

1. Unsupervised selection of K

We compared several candidate values of K using mean topic coherence (c_v) over 5 initializations (train/validation 70/30).
Rule: maximise mean validation coherence → K = 6.

Published LDA: K = 6 re-trained with many passes (~100 000), models/lda/LDA_model_vdec25.gensim.

4. Interpretation

  • K = 6 maximises mean validation coherence among the candidates evaluated on the revision embeddings.
  • Level 1 (seed): topic–profile cosine has median > 0.9; hypertopics and pUF remain highly concordant.
  • Level 2 (leave-20%-out): cosine remains high for most topics/hypertopics under patient subsampling.
  • Results are essentially unchanged vs the lock-date stability report.
